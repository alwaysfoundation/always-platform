# Project governance

This project serves the Church and the proclamation of the Gospel. Technology stays subordinate to Scripture, prayer, discipleship and the ministry of local churches; where a feature pulls people from these, it gives way.

- **Owner:** the Always Foundation, a US 501(c)(3) nonprofit. The code is Apache 2.0, so anyone can run, fork or build on it. Rejoice and UEN have stewarded the project and will assign the code, brand and related rights to the foundation.
- **Technology Steering Committee** (being formed) owns the roadmap, the Always Handoff standard and security. It is open to contributors who have shown sustained, careful work.
- **Safeguarding, Privacy and Ethics Committee** (being formed) must approve any change to consent, minors rules, retention, routing timers or AI features. It can stop any feature that puts people at risk.
- **Maintainers** review and merge. Until the committees are seated, Rejoice engineers review and approve changes. Two human approvals are required on every change; code owners on protected paths.
- **Decisions** are made in the open through issues and RFCs (`docs/rfcs/`), with significant technical choices recorded as ADRs (`docs/adr/`).
- **Commercial builders** (including Rejoice) build on the platform on the same terms as everyone else, declare conflicts, and hold no special rights.
