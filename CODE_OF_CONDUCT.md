# Code of conduct

We adopt the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1 (https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), and add one line from our own tradition: "Let all that you do be done in love" (1 Corinthians 16:14).

People of every background and tradition are welcome to contribute. We expect kindness, patience and honest, respectful disagreement. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not acceptable.

Report concerns to **conduct@alwaysfoundation.org**. Reports are handled confidentially by two maintainers who are not involved, with escalation to the foundation's board.
