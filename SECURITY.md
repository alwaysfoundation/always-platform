# Security policy

Always handles people's faith stories, which Australian privacy law treats as sensitive information. We take every report seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability
- Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting ("Report a vulnerability" under the Security tab), or email **security@alwaysfoundation.org**.
- Please don't open a public issue, and don't access, change or keep anyone's data. Use synthetic accounts.
- We acknowledge within 2 business days, give an initial assessment within 5, and aim to fix critical issues within 7 days.
- We credit researchers who want to be credited. We do not take legal action against good-faith research that follows this policy.

## Scope
The API, web apps, SDK, database policies and infrastructure configuration in this organisation's repositories. Social engineering, physical attacks and denial of service are out of scope.

## Our commitments
See the Always Foundation Cybersecurity Policy (`docs/policies/`): Essential Eight Maturity Level 2, OWASP ASVS Level 2 (Level 3 for consent, minors and access control), personal data in Australia only, field encryption, row-level security and a hash-chained audit log. Notifiable data breaches are handled under the NDB scheme.
